Calibration camera pose and focal length from the mat markers

How the matching works
  1. Each mat marker has known corners on the mat (mm). In the frame, its corners or centre are found (detected by vision/blocks.py, or clicked here).
  2. A homography maps the mat plane to the image (normalised DLT, at least 4 points).
  3. The homography gives the focal length (its two rotation columns must be orthogonal and of equal length) and a first pose.
  4. Levenberg-Marquardt then minimises the reprojection error over pose, focal length and principal point.
  5. Orange squares are the markers reprojected with the fitted camera, white circles the measured points, blue crosses their reprojection, red lines the error (5× enlarged).
